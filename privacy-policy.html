Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

How EcoScrap collects, uses and safeguards your information across pickups, payments and support.

1 · Overview

Data We Collect

EcoScrap ("we", "us") collects the minimum data needed to run a pickup: your name, contact number, email, pickup address, transaction records and — where you choose to provide it — payment detail for settlements. This policy explains what we hold, why we hold it and the controls you have.

  • Account & profile data — name, phone, email, saved addresses.
  • Transaction data — pickup requests, weights, rates, payouts.
  • Usage data — device, browser and pages visited for service improvement.
  • Communications — support interactions and newsletter preferences.
2 · Use

How Your Data Is Used

We use your information to book pickups, schedule collectors, settle payments, send status updates, meet regulatory documentation obligations and — with your consent — share rates and tips. We do not sell personal data, and we never share it for advertising-based activity.

Legal grounds

Processing is grounded in contract (providing the pickup), legal obligation (E-Waste Management Rules returns), consent (marketing) and legitimate interest (fraud prevention).

3 · Sharing

Who Sees Your Data

Limited categories of recipients may access data strictly on a need-to-know basis: payment processors, certified recycling facilities (for consignment manifests), logistics partners and government authorities where legally required. Every processor signs a data-protection agreement.

4 · Security

Security, Retention & Your Rights

Transmission is encrypted in transit and at rest; access is role-scoped and audited. Transaction records are retained as required by tax and e-waste rules, then securely deleted or anonymised.

You may access, rectify, restrict processing of, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise any right, email compliance@ecoscrap.in or write to 42, Green Avenue, Okhla, New Delhi 110020. This page also acts as our demo privacy statement for this front-end prototype.

Questions About This Document?

Our compliance team is happy to clarify — reach out any day between 7 AM and 9 PM.
