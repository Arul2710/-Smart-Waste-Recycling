Our Story

Founded on a Simple Frustration

In 2024 we started EcoScrap after watching neighbours haul heavy scrap bags to the nearest dealer — where weights were guessed, rates were opaque and payment was later than promised. We built the service we wished existed: a booked slot, a calibrated scale and cash on the spot.

Today our fleet of collection vehicles serves six metros, and every item we pick up is sorted and routed to certified recyclers with a recorded handover chain.

Responsibility

Every Kilogram Goes to a Certified Destination

Recycling can be greenwashing if the chain isn't audited. We track each consignment from your doorstep to the receiver, and share the summary on request.

  • Handover manifestsConsignment-level records available in your dashboard.
  • Audited facilitiesPartner recyclers licensed under E-Waste and Hazardous Waste rules.
  • Closed-loop reportingWe measure energy saved, water saved and emissions avoided.
